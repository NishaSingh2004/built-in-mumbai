Our Story

2018: The Fragmented City
Mumbai had the talent, the capital, and the hunger, but it lacked a center of gravity. Founders were building in silos, disconnected from the very community that could accelerate them.
2021: Midnight Hacks & Shared Ambition
What started as a single dinner for ten founders turned into a movement. We realized that the ultimate leverage is trusted community. Late-night sessions in Bandra and BKC became the foundation for something real to begin.
2024: The Connective Tissue
Today, Build in Mumbai is the high-octane engine for the city's most ambitious builders. We are more than a club; we are the infrastructure for the next generation of Indian enterprise.

OUR MISSION

To aggressively accelerate the density of innovation in Mumbai.

Mumbai isn't just about finance and Bollywood; we are a city of relentless builders. Our mission is to lower the friction required for collaboration, knowledge-sharing, and brutal, honest feedback among the city's best.
